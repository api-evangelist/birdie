# Birdie

> Birdie is a UK homecare technology platform that unifies care management, rostering, scheduling, finance and invoicing, quality and compliance, medication and clinically-validated assessments into one system, with dedicated apps for carers and families. Over 1,000 homecare providers use Birdie. Birdie does not publish a public developer API or OpenAPI specification; integration is handled through raw-data access for business-intelligence tooling and partner arrangements.

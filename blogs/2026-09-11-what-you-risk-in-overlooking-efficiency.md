Efficiency isn't just a buzzword; it's a cornerstone of successful care delivery. For owners and managers of care agencies, overlooking efficiency or pushing it to the back burner can lead to a multitude of challenges, jeopardising both the quality of care provided and the sustainability of the business. This article explores the risks associated with overlooking efficiency in key operational areas and offers practical steps to mitigate these risks.‍‍
